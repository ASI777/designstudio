# Sports-car visual inspiration set

These photographs are reference-only inputs for DesignStudio's industrial
design workflow. They may inform abstract attributes such as proportion,
surface tension, layering, control hierarchy, material contrast, and color
blocking. They are not geometry sources.

## Intended influence

- `porsche-911t-front.jpg`: compact visual mass, soft continuous curvature,
  restrained detailing, and a clear symmetric hierarchy.
- `mclaren-artura-rear.jpg`: technical vent language, strong corner treatment,
  layered lower structure, and a visually light upper volume.
- `lotus-emira-rear.jpg`: tapered plan view, controlled surface transitions,
  thin graphic lighting line, and planted stance.
- `alpine-a110-interior.jpg`: driver-focused control grouping, material
  contrast, recessed control zones, and a layered console.

## Do not copy

Do not reproduce vehicle silhouettes, lamps, grilles, vents, badges, logos,
surface graphics, upholstery patterns, or brand-specific control arrangements.
The controller must retain an original enclosure silhouette, control treatment,
surface language, and CMF identity.

See `manifest.json` for source, author, license, dimensions, and SHA-256
provenance. CC BY-SA images require attribution and share-alike compliance when
the photographs themselves or adaptations of them are redistributed.
